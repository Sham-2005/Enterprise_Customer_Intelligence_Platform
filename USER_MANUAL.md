# ECIP BI Dashboard User Manual

Welcome to the **Enterprise Customer Intelligence Platform (ECIP)** Executive BI Dashboard.

## Dashboard Navigation Pages

1. **Executive Dashboard**: C-suite overview of total revenue, orders, customers, AOV, retention/churn rates, and monthly trends.
2. **Customer Analytics**: Active vs Inactive ratios, repeat purchase rate, customer state breakdown, and age distributions.
3. **AI Customer Segmentation**: 3D PCA cluster scatter plots, business persona strategy blueprints (*VIP Power Buyers*, *Loyal Frequenters*), and model comparison tables.
4. **Customer Risk Intelligence**: Real-time churn prediction sandbox, SHAP plain-English explanations, 5-tier risk roster, and batch CSV prediction uploader.
5. **Revenue Intelligence & CLV**: 12-month future CLV projections, Platinum/Gold value tier leaderboards, and revenue opportunity blueprints.
6. **AI Recommendation Engine**: Personalized customer product recommendation sandbox, item-item similarity lookup, and trending products.
7. **Market Basket Analysis**: Support vs Confidence scatter plots, category co-occurrence heatmaps, and high-value product bundle strategies.
8. **MLOps & AI Governance**: Central model registry, version rollback controls, experiment logs, and Kolmogorov-Smirnov data drift audits.
9. **Sales Analytics**: Revenue by payment method, seasonal distribution, and category treemaps.
10. **Product Analytics**: Pareto 80/20 revenue concentration curves and product rankings.
11. **Order Analytics**: Delivery duration box plots and fulfillment status breakdowns.
12. **Review Analytics**: CSAT rating distributions and multi-category radar benchmarks.
13. **Seller Analytics**: Merchant leaderboard rankings and seller location maps.
14. **Entity Search Engine**: Instant lookup by Customer ID, Order ID, Product ID, Seller ID, or Category.
